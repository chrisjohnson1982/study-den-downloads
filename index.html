Study Den

Year 5 practice for maths, English, French, geography, science and history, with coins and rewards set by a grown-up.

Download for Android Download for Windows

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on the phone or tablet.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install (or Update). Installing over an older version keeps coins, stars and the family link.

Windows

  1. Download and open StudyDen-Setup.exe. No administrator password is needed.
  2. If SmartScreen says it “protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Follow the steps. Running a newer installer later upgrades it and keeps the family link.

The app does nothing until it joins a family with a code from a grown-up’s device.
